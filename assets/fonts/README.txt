Locally hosted Google Fonts

Manrope — variable clean sans, weights 200–800.
Anton — condensed display face, weight 400. Avoid synthetic bold.

Use the adjacent fonts.css, or copy its @font-face rules into the site stylesheet and update URL paths relative to that stylesheet.
Suggested body: font-family: 'Manrope', sans-serif;
Suggested display: font-family: 'Anton', sans-serif; font-weight: 400; text-transform: uppercase;
