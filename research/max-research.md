## Original short editorial copy

**The driver:** Born into a racing family, Max Verstappen reached Formula 1 at 17. A year later, he won on his Red Bull Racing debut in Barcelona. Four consecutive world championships followed between 2021 and 2024.

**The instinct:** From karting circuits to the world stage, his story is built around one enduring pursuit: finding the limit, then going further.

**Beyond the grid:** Racing continues away from Formula 1. Verstappen's interests span the simulator, GT competition and endurance racing. His racing initiative connects those worlds and supports the progression from virtual competition to real circuits.

Supporting sources: [Formula 1 Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-max-verstappen.2DttvntAuFvHYrBY2tV8fN), [Red Bull's profile](https://www.redbullracing.com/int-en/everything-to-know-about-max-verstappen-2026), [Verstappen Racing](https://racing.verstappen.com/about/).

## Timeline copy

- **2015 — A new generation.** Formula 1 debut with Toro Rosso in Australia, aged 17.
- **2016 — History, rewritten.** Victory in Spain on his Red Bull debut. The youngest Grand Prix winner.
- **2021 — The first crown.** His first world title is decided at the Abu Dhabi finale. [F1 Hall of Fame](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/drivers-hall-of-fame-max-verstappen.2DttvntAuFvHYrBY2tV8fN)
- **2022 — Back to back.** A second championship, secured at Suzuka. [F1 Japanese GP report](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/verstappen-crowned-world-champion-with-japanese-gp-victory-after-late.7g1xgGHHew3f2If32u8U1z.7g1xgGHHew3f2If32u8U1z)
- **2023 — A season apart.** A third title and 19 Grand Prix wins. [F1 records review](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/in-numbers-the-remarkable-records-verstappen-and-red-bull-broke-during-the.5rU6XbfSRwp1wH9Uzp4RxI)
- **2024 — Four in a row.** A fourth consecutive championship, secured in Las Vegas. [FIA race report](https://www.fia.com/news/f1-verstappen-seals-fourth-title-vegas-russell-takes-dominant-win)
